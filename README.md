World: ~4.4 km across, one forest biome. The Chapter 1 valley sits in the middle inside a ring of ridges; roads leave it through passes
to the outlands (Varnhold, Sunscar, Elderhollow clan holds, Cinder City, the Watchers, Lake Varr, Mirrorwater). Terrain chunks and
outland forest cells stream in around the player (`updTerrain` / `updForest` in `1b-build.js`).

Kestrel (Nov 11 layout): the wall is no longer a circle — it runs in straight, uneven stretches (`WALL_PTS` in 1-world.js), 64 m out at the gate, tight behind the Arc and to the west, and out to ~96 m on the east side. `wallR(a)` gives its distance at an angle, `campD(x, z)` how far out a point is scaled so the wall is always at `CAMP_R` (use it wherever you'd have compared `Math.hypot(x, z)` with `CAMP_R`), `wallPt(a, off)` a point on it. Raiders climb it wherever it is and walk round it to the gate. In through the gate, Main Street wanders up to the Arc's door: on the left the Gunsmith, Outfitter and Trading Post, then West Lane opens off to the Clinic, the Workshop and the Storehouse round the back; on the right the Cookhouse and Bunkhouse, then East Row branches off right to the commons fire and power yard, Salvage, the Munitions shed (Vesna: ammo + explosives, a powder bench, behind blast walls and sandbags) and the Depot up Foundry Lane.

Previously: the wall was 64 m out. The Arc (the main hull) is walk-in — E at its door seals/opens the blast doors; a ramp inside leads to a catwalk with a firing slit over the yard. Raiders who get over the wall go for the Arc: if it's sealed they hammer the doors, if they get in they go for the core. Settlers shelter inside at night. Main Street runs from the gate to the Arc's door; Arc Row, Cross Street, West Lane and East Lane split the yard into blocks, and each building faces a street. Seven shops, each with a keeper (E: trade for scrap, Q: their extra — Kade upgrades, Juno patches, Rook daily ammo) and a station at the side (cook, craft + forge, materials, medicine, weapons, clothing). Gus at Salvage pays 75% for what you sell (everyone else 50%). The Bunkhouse lets you rest to full health. Shop stock refreshes daily and better gear appears as your tier rises. Six named settlers wander the yard and barter what they've gathered.

Start: you wake at the dropsite (south-west, ~400 m out) beside a section of the Arc torn off on the way down — loot the wreck, then follow the old road north-east to Kestrel, or dig in and build there. Kestrel's night raids start once you've reached it.

The Arc (Nov 10): the hull is dented, wavy and patched, with bent and snapped ribs; its east end carries on through a buckled joint into the east wing, which bends round to the south and is walk-in too (`WING`, `inWing`, `hullOff` in `1c-camp.js`). The streets and buildings sit a little off square.

The Rust (red fog, Nov 10): the first one comes ~45 s after you first reach Kestrel (day 1), then every 7–10 days. The fog clears inside the hull. It hurts more the longer you're out (~5 hp/s rising to ~15). A roof does not stop it: only the sealed hull counts as cover (out in the world: gas mask, hazmat, breather, Rust medicine). Everyone in town runs for the Arc; one or two get caught, go down, and die ~70 s later unless you treat them (E, uses a bandage / medkit / Rust medicine). `fogNPC` in `3-game.js`.

Night 100 is announced over the radio: from day 70 Farlight (and once Halvard) call Kestrel as the clans gather (`RADIO_CALLS` in 3-game.js). Carry a radio — take a walkie-talkie from the rack in the Arc's radio room (end of the east wing) — and you hear each call at dawn; otherwise they wait on the radio desk in the Arc (E to answer).

Open world: no chapters. Tier keys: clan insignia (2), Arc ID chip (3), Greyhold card (4), Dr. Arden's notebook (5), Gate Stone codes (6). Nights bring raids (clans, then Ironjaw from night 6, Greyhold from night 20); night 100 is the big one. Dying drops your bag where you fell.

Movement has momentum (accelerate/brake over a fraction of a second, slide along trees, steep climbs slow you), jump buffering + coyote time, and a stamina-limited sprint (bar above health; run it dry and you're winded until a third comes back).

Settings (Nov 10): title menu → Settings, the Settings tab in the bag, or O. **Textures** Low / Medium / High (`TEX_LEVELS` in 1-world.js: model texture cap 512 / 1024 / full, sharpness, phone or PC ground textures; applies on reload, the game saves first). **View distance** Near 300 m / Medium 450 m / Far 700 m / Max no limit (`VIEW_LEVELS`, `setViewDist` in 3-game.js; live): a fog wall closes everything in at that distance (packed into `fogNear` — see `packFogNear`), and terrain chunks, forest cells, props, districts, far forest and smoke past it aren't streamed or drawn; the 9 km horizon ranges only draw at Max. Saved in localStorage `arc-gfx`; `?tex=` / `?view=` in the URL override. Defaults: PC High + Far, phones Low + Medium.

Phone set (Nov 11, KTX2): Textures: Low (the phone default) loads every model from `assets/models-m/`, made offline from `assets/models/` by `tools/ktx.mjs`. Its textures are GPU-compressed KTX2 (Basis Universal), which stay compressed in graphics memory (transcoded to ASTC/ETC on iPhones, BC7 on PCs by `KTX2Loader`, `vendor/jsm/libs/basis/`): colour maps 1024 px on the guns, arms and people and 512 px on everything else (ETC1S), normal maps 512 px (UASTC + RDO + zstd), roughness/metal/AO 512 px (ETC1S); anything over 20k triangles cut toward it. Every texture is twice as sharp as the first (512/256 px RGBA) phone set for about the same graphics memory (~250 MB at most in testing), and no full-size image is ever decoded on the phone. Low also uses the PC ground/metal textures (1024 px) with 4x filtering and draws at up to 1.5x pixel density (`adaptRes` still drops it when the frame rate sags). Phones unpack at most four model files at once (`loadGLB` in 1-world.js). Download: ~86 MB of models. PC set: Medium and High read `assets/models-p/` — every colour texture at full size, only the normal/roughness/metal/AO maps halved (1024 px on the close-up list, 512 px on the rest): ~2.5 GB → ~1.5 GB of GPU textures, no visible change.

**After adding or replacing a model**, rebuild both copies (needs `npm i @gltf-transform/core @gltf-transform/extensions @gltf-transform/functions meshoptimizer sharp ktx2-encoder` once; only files newer than their copy are redone, `--force` redoes all):
```
CLOSE='^(w/|fp-arms|keeper|settler|civilian|michael|elias|gun-|sniper|spare/)'
node tools/ktx.mjs assets/models assets/models-m --color 1024 --detail 512 --tris 20000 --match "$CLOSE"
node tools/ktx.mjs assets/models assets/models-m --color 512 --detail 512 --tris 20000 --skip "$CLOSE"
node tools/mobile.mjs assets/models assets/models-p --color 4096 --detail 1024 --tris 999999999 --match "$CLOSE"
node tools/mobile.mjs assets/models assets/models-p --color 4096 --detail 512 --tris 999999999 --skip "$CLOSE"
```

Performance: hand-placed props are distance-culled by size (`buildCull`/`updCull` in `3-game.js`, hidden ones go to layer 1 so neither the camera nor the shadow pass draws them); tiny props don't cast shadows; resolution adapts to hold frame rate (`adaptRes`).
More performance: `bakeWorld` (3-game.js) merges static props that share a look into one mesh per group (~1,900 fewer draw calls; anything that moves, hides or flickers is skipped, and the wall bakes itself in `buildWall`). The point lights stay in the scene but invisible, and a pool of 4 real lights copies whichever ones reach the camera (`buildLightPool`/`updLightPool`) — the light count is fixed, so no shader recompiles. People swap to `*-lo.glb` (~1.5k triangles) past ~25 m, and enemies carry the low-poly guns. Deer/boar parts are fused per moving piece. Tone mapping happens in the grade pass (no OutputPass), and `adaptRes` drops bloom if the floor resolution still isn't enough.

## Performance (Nov 12) — `src/1a-perf.js`

Measured before touching anything (`tools/perf/bench.py`: headless Chrome, a new game, eight fixed camera spots + an explosion; draw calls and triangles are counted across every pass of a frame — shadow map, scene, bloom, grade — and averaged over two frames because the shadow map redraws every other frame). Where it went: 7–10 million triangles a frame around Kestrel, about half of them in the shadow pass, almost all from a handful of imported models drawn at full detail at any distance — the conifers (5.1k triangles each, ~600 of them), the hero conifers (30k), Davia's rock outcrops (~44k each), the wrecked cars (20k) — and whole 200–300 m forest bins redrawn into a 120 m shadow map.

What changed (nothing about the art, the models, the textures or the gameplay):
- **Instanced LOD** (`ilodParts`, used automatically by `instParts` for anything ≥1.5k triangles): each instance is drawn at full / medium / far detail by distance (full detail to ~22–35 m, medium to ~80–150 m, scaled by model size and the preset), with a few metres of hysteresis so nothing flickers on a boundary. The medium/far meshes are made at load by meshoptimizer's simplifier (`lodGeo`, `vendor/jsm/libs/meshopt_simplifier.module.js`, v1.3) weighing normals and UVs, so they keep the shape within ~1% and the texture in place — same vertices, same materials, nothing new to download.
- **Per-instance frustum culling**: instances off screen aren't drawn (checked when the camera moves 1.5 m or turns ~2°, with a margin); nothing past the view distance exists.
- **Shadow proxies**: shadows come from shadow-only copies (layer 3, `SHADOW_ONLY`, which only the sun's shadow camera sees) holding just the instances close enough to land a shadow in the 120 m shadow map, drawn with the medium mesh. The drawn fir/alder/snag forest (valley buckets and outland cells) gets the same (`shnAdd`): the buckets no longer cast shadows themselves.
- **Underground occlusion** (`updUnder`): the pockets sit 150 m under the map's corners, so while you're in one the ground, forests, grass and far scenery aren't drawn.
- **Pools**: particles are one instanced mesh (one draw for all of them; they were a new mesh + material each — 34 per explosion), tracers are a fixed set of 24 lines, arrows/bolts/spears/grenades and the clans' thrown spears are reused (each one used to leave its geometry on the GPU).
- **Effects + sound**: puffs more than 120 m away spawn a third as many (slightly bigger) bits and none past 300 m; at most 32 sounds (20 on phones) play at once, so a firefight can't pile up hundreds of overlapping voices.
- **Shared materials**: tinted model copies share one material per (material, tint) and the valley cliffs one tinted copy, so the bake can merge them.
- **Lights**: a siege fire's light joins the light pool (a new live light recompiled every lit material mid-raid); a light taken out of the world no longer lights from where it was.
- **Graphics memory** (`updResidency`): textures/meshes nothing visible has used for 30 s are released from the GPU when the estimate is over budget (phones ~320 MB, computers ~1.4 GB), or after 10 minutes unused; they re-upload by themselves if you come back. Outland forest cells share one rock mesh (each cell leaked one).
- **Shader warm-up** (`warmShaders`): every material is compiled behind the loading screen ("WARMING UP") instead of the first time it comes into view — the hitch the first time you walked into each building or saw each prop.
- **Culling**: `updCull` has 6 m of hysteresis; anything flagged `userData.landmark` (or over 30 m across) draws to the view distance.

**Graphics presets** (title → Settings, or the bag's Settings tab): **Performance / Balanced / Quality**, tuned separately for computers and phones (`PRESETS` in 1-world.js). A preset sets Textures + View distance (still adjustable on their own — the preset then reads "Custom") plus shadow-map size and redraw rate, bloom, the resolution ceiling/floor `adaptRes` works between, grass density, LOD distances and shadow reach. Defaults keep today's look exactly: computers **Quality** (High textures, Far view, 2048 shadows, bloom, up to 1.75x pixels), phones **Balanced** (Low = KTX2 textures, Medium view, 1024 shadows, up to 1.5x). Performance: computers Medium textures / Medium view / 1024 shadows every 3rd frame / no bloom / 1.25x; phones Near view / 1024 shadows every 3rd frame / 1.2x / 60% grass. `?preset=perf|bal|qual` in the URL overrides. Saved with the rest in localStorage `arc-gfx`.

**Stats overlay**: F3, or Settings → Performance stats (`?stats` in the URL): FPS + 1% low, frame time, draw calls and triangles (all passes), estimated graphics memory (uploaded textures + mesh buffers), texture/geometry/shader counts, live particles / people + animals / sounds, shader warm-up time, resolution, preset, load time + bytes, and what the memory manager has released. `window.__perf.snap()` returns the same numbers.

**Measuring** (needs `pip install playwright` and Chrome): `python3 tools/perf/bench.py <folder> <port> <label> pc|phone` writes `<label>-<mode>.json` + screenshots; `python3 tools/perf/diag.py <folder> <port> x z lookX lookZ` lists the meshes costing the most triangles from a spot. `?bench` holds the resolution still (no `adaptRes`) so runs compare. SwiftShader renders on the CPU, so frame times only compare run-to-run on one machine; draw calls, triangles and memory are exact.

## Gentler survival (Nov 11, Part 3)

Survival backs up the adventure instead of interrupting it. Tuning knobs: `SURV`, `BUFF_DEF` and `DUR_K` in `2a-data.js`; logic in `updSurvival` / `repairGear` (2b-craft.js).
- Food drains ~3× slower than before (~65 min of walking from full). Starving wears health down to 12 and stops, it never kills. Natural healing starts at 35 food.
- Water is its own light bar (~45 min from full, saved with the weather): drink at any lake or river ("Drink", small chance of an upset stomach) or from water you carry (slot 4 drinks first when you're thirsty). Running dry never hurts — it just stops natural healing and halves stamina refill until you drink.
- Cooked food gives buffs: **Energised** (fish, bread, clean water: sprinting costs half, stamina refills faster), **Mending** (stews, soups, honey, roast: heal over time), **Hardy** (cooked/dried meat, roast, stew: −20% damage, a bit of red-fog resistance). Raw food is less risky and sickness is shorter.
- Fishing: better odds, a rod can land two, fish bite more in rain and fog. Water: rainwater is clean, rain fills a carried pot or canteen, purification tablets craft from charcoal + herb, rainwater filters without charcoal.
- Gear lasts 2.5× longer (old saves' gear is stretched once on load). Repair from the bag (per item, or the **Repair all** button by the gear list, most worn first — both only appear once something is below 40% condition): a repair kit fixes fully anywhere; otherwise a few scrap (or a stick + 2 fiber for primitive tools) patches to 60%, or fully at a workbench/forge. You get a warning when something is wearing out.
Exploration (Nov 11, Part 1): Millbrook (walked-out farm village; the Rourke cellar under the bottom house, with a hidden room behind a shelf), KVRN relay station on the south rim (the numbers tape), Kestrel Ridge trail (cairns from behind the camp to the summit, then down to Whisper Cave — a painted hall, an underground pool, a floor that gives way into the deep chamber with the thirteen marks), the Coldwater Line (a 240 m rail tunnel through the east rim: west portal in the valley, east portal outside; fuse in the alcove powers the maintenance bay), Fort Hesper (old army post, Ironjaw squatters, respawns like the other bases; the bunker under it: power the generator, the colonel's drawer has the armory key and the Brightwater keycard), Brightwater Annex (the lab under it: decon, specimen labs, a hot room, the archive behind the keycard), the Long Fall (Hydroponics Ring C, crashed decades ago; the crew locker's ID chip opens the med stores), Route 9 (a jammed highway with a chained trailer and a fallen overpass), Ember Rest (a burnt clan camp), the Black Door (SITE 9, AWAITING SIGNAL — not openable yet), eight little camps, six marked stashes, the rope bridge and the stepping stones. 39 logs in all; the Journal tab lists what you've found and which places are still out there.
